# Propuesta landing: Centro Infantil Dejando Huella

Scope: Persuade. Proposal by Gloz Studio for the owner of Centro Infantil Dejando Huella (Ciudad Quesada, CR): short cover with before/after, then the full landing mockup. Mobile first. One action: WhatsApp to schedule a visit. Unknown facts shown as labelled sample values; stock photos labelled "foto ilustrativa".

Delivery order (Ruva Web Pro gates): 1) `propuesta/direccion-de-arte.html` art-direction sheet, approved before building; 2) section structure + headline copy approval; 3) build `propuesta/index.html` + LEEME.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Cada niño deja su retazo. The landing is one hand-pieced quilt where every block answers a question a parent asks before calling. It refuses the category default: cream-pastel page, centered hero over a row of same-size rounded cards with cartoon icons.

OWN-WORLD: Patches tiled edge to edge over a navy denim backing (#0D1A3C, logo navy). Fabrics are the logo's colours rendered as clean CSS weaves: coral gingham (#E38F83), chambray (#91A8CB), mustard stripe (#DCA74C), sage felt (#A6B3A6), navy denim, and manta white (#FBF7EF) reserved for reading patches. Coral running-stitch seams on every edge; photos stitched on as patches; handwritten cloth labels in Shantell Sans over Nunito body; navy ink on every colour. Buttons are embroidered patches. Depth only as fabric lift.

STORY: A parent on a phone sees real children in care, the practical facts, a day in the centre, the visit steps, and believes this is a careful, warm local place; they tap WhatsApp to schedule a visit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 375px: logo patch bar; a five-patch quilt block: manta label patch with "Donde cada pequeño paso cuenta", photo patch of children painting, handprint patch, sample-hours patch; embroidered coral "Agenda una visita" patch fully visible at 375x812. 1440px: label patch 5/12 left holding headline and CTA, tall photo patch 7/12 right, fact patches stitched beneath.

FORM: memory quilt atlas (catalog world textiles-weave-drape-fashion-memory-quilt-atlas), lead of the bolder re-roll round 1. Signature: a coral stitch sews the hero patches together and runs on to outline the CTA patch; a tap leaves a fading handprint. seed:d45c001ba9303c40

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
